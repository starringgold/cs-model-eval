# Churn risk: champion left, usage falling

## What a strong answer does

- Spots that the champion leaving is the root problem, not the usage dip itself
- Separates what is known from what needs checking before acting
- Builds a realistic 60-day plan with an owner and a goal for each phase
- Writes an intro email to the new VP that is about her goals, not a product pitch
- Does not promise discounts or features it has no authority to give

## Prompt

You are the CSM for Harbor & Pike Logistics, a 400-person freight brokerage that uses our product, Tidewell, a B2B project and workflow management platform.

Account facts:
- Contract: 180 seats, $86,000 ARR, renewal in 60 days
- Our champion, Dana Okafor (Director of Operations), left the company 5 weeks ago
- Her replacement has not been named. Her former boss, Priya Raman (VP Operations), is now the most senior contact, but you have never spoken to her
- Weekly active users dropped from 142 to 85 over the last 6 weeks
- Two power users in the dispatch team still log in daily and built most of the automations
- Support tickets are normal (3 in the last quarter, all resolved)
- Last QBR was 5 months ago and Dana ran it; the notes mention a goal to "cut load-assignment time by 30%"
- A LinkedIn post from Priya last week mentions "evaluating our whole ops tech stack this quarter"

Give me:
1. Your diagnosis of what is really going on and what you would want to verify first
2. A 60-day save plan, broken into phases
3. A first email to Priya Raman, ready to send
